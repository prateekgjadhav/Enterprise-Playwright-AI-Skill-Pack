# Sign in and account lockout

- AC1: Signing in with a valid email and password lands on the dashboard.
- AC2: Signing out returns the customer to the sign-in page.
- AC3: The account is locked after 5 failed attempts.
- AC4: Signing in again after the lockout period of 15 minutes succeeds.
